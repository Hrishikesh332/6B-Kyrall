For additive manufacturing, distinguish geometry validity from printability.
Check build volume after applying the intended print orientation. Discuss supports,
layer direction, thin features, holes, fit allowances, and fastener access. Keep
moving parts separate and choose clearance according to the stated process; do not
promise a universal fit allowance. Include a small fit-test recommendation when
interfaces depend on printer calibration. Document whether springs, fasteners,
fixtures, elastomer pads, or other nonprinted components are omitted.
Our built-in checks do not perform wall-thickness sampling, overhang screening,
structural analysis, or toolpath generation. Do not claim those checks were run.
Material selection in this backend is descriptive, not a validated material model.
